# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| latest | ✅ |

Only the currently deployed services, web apps and the latest store build of
our mobile apps are supported. Older app builds are fixed by updating.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub Issues,
pull requests or discussions.** Anything filed publicly is visible to everyone
and may be acted on by automation before a person reads it.

Email **central.inbox@dustwhisperers.com** with `SECURITY` in the subject line.

Include the following in your report:
- Description of the vulnerability and the affected URL, app or repository
- Steps to reproduce (requests, payloads, screenshots)
- Potential impact
- Any suggested fixes (optional)

Please do **not** include real customer or contractor personal data, passwords,
tokens or payment details in the report. If you came across credentials, tell
us where they are rather than copying them.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 48 hours.
- We aim to give an initial assessment (confirmed / need more information /
  not an issue) within 7 days.
- Updates as the fix progresses, and a note when it is resolved.

We ask that you give us reasonable time to fix the issue before any public
disclosure, and we will agree a disclosure date with you.

## Scope

In scope: the Dust Whisperers web properties under `dustwhisperers.com` and
its subdomains, their APIs, the Clean Smart and Clean Smart Admin mobile apps,
and the public repositories in this organisation.

Out of scope: denial-of-service or load testing, social engineering or
phishing of staff, contractors or customers, physical attacks, spam, reports
from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact, and issues in
third-party services we use (report those to the vendor).

## Rules of engagement

- Use only accounts you created yourself, and stop as soon as you can show the
  issue. Do not access, modify or delete data that does not belong to you.
- Do not make or attempt real bookings, payments, refunds, payouts or bank
  changes, and do not trigger SMS or email to people other than yourself.
- Do not degrade the service for other users.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith and follow this policy, we will not pursue or support
legal action against you for your research, and we will work with you to
understand and fix the issue. We are committed to working with security
researchers and will acknowledge your contribution once the issue is resolved,
if you wish.
